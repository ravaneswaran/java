Question
========
how to know the price rise of a stock before the market starts?

Chat GPT Response
=================

Excellent question — and a very practical one if you’re studying trading or automation 📈

Let’s break it down step-by-step:

🎯 Goal
--------

You want to know:

“Which stocks are likely to rise in price before the market actually opens.”

That means you want to estimate or observe pre-market price movement or signals before 9:15 AM (NSE) or 9:30 AM (BSE) in India.

🕐 Market Timing Refresher (India)
----------------------------------
Session                                  Time (IST)          Notes
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Pre-open session                         9:00 AM – 9:08 AM   Orders are placed; indicative prices are formed.
Order matching (pre-open equilibrium)    9:08 AM – 9:12 AM   NSE matches demand/supply and calculates equilibrium price.
Buffer / transition                      9:12 AM – 9:15 AM   Orders frozen; final price discovery pushed to regular market.
Market opens                             9:15 AM             Regular trading begins.

So:
👉 The price rise signal before market open comes from the pre-open market (9:00–9:12 AM).

🔍 Step 1 — Watch the “Pre-Open Market” Data

Official source:
🔗 https://www.nseindia.com/market-data/pre-open-market-cm-and-emerge-market

During 9:00–9:12 AM, you’ll see for each stock:

Indicative equilibrium price

Previous close

Difference (%)

Buy/sell quantities

📊 How to read it
------------------

If a stock’s indicative equilibrium price is higher than its previous close —
→ there’s demand pressure (expected rise).

Example:

Symbol  Prev Close  Equilibrium Price   Change (%)  Trend
----------------------------------------------------------
INFY    1500        1530                +2.0%       Likely upward open
TCS     3650        3640                -0.3%       Flat/down


🧠 Step 2 — Analyze “Gap Up” and “Gap Down” Stocks
---------------------------------------------------

After 9:08 AM, many broker terminals and sites publish:

Top Gainers (Pre-open)

Top Losers (Pre-open)

These lists show which stocks are likely to open higher or lower.

Examples:

https://www.moneycontrol.com/stocks/marketstats/preopen/
https://www.nseindia.com/live_market/dynaContent/live_watch/pre_open_market/pre_open_market.htm
https://www.screener.in

---------------------------------------------------------

💬 Step 4 — Optional: Use Global / Derivative Indicators
--------------------------------------------------------

Sometimes, prices rise due to overnight cues, even before pre-open begins.

You can check:

Source                                                Data Type                            Purpose
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SGX Nifty / GIFT Nifty                                Index futures (before 9:00 AM)       Market direction hint
US markets (Dow/Nasdaq)                               Overnight trends                     Sentiment correlation
ADR prices (for Indian companies listed abroad)       e.g., INFY ADR on NYSE               Estimate open gap
FII/DII data                                          Previous day’s flows                 Institutional trend


⚙️ Step 5 — Combine Clues
Time                   What You Can Observe              Likely Signal
----------------------------------------------------------------------
8:30 AM                GIFT Nifty, global markets        Market-wide sentiment
9:00–9:08 AM           Pre-open orders building up       Stock-level interest
9:08–9:12 AM           Indicative equilibrium prices     Likely open gaps
9:15 AM                Market opens                      Confirmation


🚫 Note

* Before 9:00 AM, no live stock price exists — only cues from derivatives, ADRs, or sentiment.
* The real actionable “price before open” comes from 9:00–9:12 AM pre-open session.
* Predictions outside that (overnight) are just statistical or sentiment guesses.
