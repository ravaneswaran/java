how to calculate the stoploss for Expected Target?
--------------------------------------------------

Stoploss is calculated first by defining risk, then placing SL at the nearest logical invalidity point. For an Expected Target, the common systematic ways:

1. Percentage-Based Stoploss
----------------------------
If you want to risk X% from your entry:

Stoploss = Entry Price × (1 − X/100)

Example:
Entry = 250, Risk = 2%

SL = 250 × 0.98 = 245

2. ATR-Based Stoploss (Volatility Stop)
---------------------------------------
ATR shows average price noise. Place SL outside the normal noise range:

Stoploss = Entry Price − (ATR × k)

Typical k = 1.5 or 2.

Example:
Entry = 250, ATR = 6, k = 1.5

SL = 250 − (6 × 1.5) = 241

This prevents getting stopped out on routine fluctuations.

3. Swing Low / Support-Based Stoploss
--------------------------------------
This is the most logical stoploss for trending entries:

Stoploss = Recent Swing Low (for long trades)

Example:
Entry = 250
Nearest swing low = 243

SL = 243

This means: your trade idea is wrong if price breaks below that low.

4. Risk-Reward Check (Very Important)
-------------------------------------
Before entering a trade:

Risk = Entry Price − Stoploss
Reward = Target Price − Entry Price
Risk:Reward must be ≥ 1:2 (good trades)

Example:
Entry = 250
Target = 264
SL = 243

Risk = 250 − 243 = 7
Reward = 264 − 250 = 14
R:R = 14 / 7 = 1:2   → Acceptable

If R:R < 1:1.5, skip the trade.


Summary
--------
Method	                When to Use	                          Formula
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
% Stoploss	            Intraday / quick trades	              SL = Entry × (1 − %/100)
ATR Stoploss	        Stocks with volatile movement	      SL = Entry − (ATR × 1.5 or 2)
Swing Low SL	        Swing trading & trend following	      SL = Nearest swing low
Risk-Reward Check	    Always	                              Reward ≥ 2 × Risk
